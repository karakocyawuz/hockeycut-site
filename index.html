Video analysis for ice hockey · runs on your Mac

Don't rewatch the whole game. Routine clips come ready, you focus on what matters.

Add the recorded game. HockeyCut finds goals, power plays and penalty kills, faceoffs, zone entries and D-zone exits and prepares them as short clips. Shots and turnovers come as candidates, and you answer Yes or No. Moments you want to see yourself, like the forecheck, you add yourself. You don't have to rewatch the whole game or keep pausing the video.

      The analysis happens on your Mac; the full game is never uploaded.

      Analysis runs locally on Apple Silicon; the full game video is not uploaded anywhere. Even a 4K video is converted to 1080p right on your computer.

      • Local analysis

        The model runs on your Mac. No upload queue, no waiting on someone else's servers.

      • Your answers help train the model

        That is what you give back for the free or $10 plan: your Yes and No answers and short clips of those moments are sent to us to improve our model. They are used only for training and stored encrypted; never sold or published.

      • One confirmation, once

        When you first open the app you confirm that you own the video or have the owner's permission, and that the people shown (their guardians, for players under 18) agreed to its use. You are not asked again.

      • No face recognition

        Players are not identified from their faces. Youth footage gets extra protection.
