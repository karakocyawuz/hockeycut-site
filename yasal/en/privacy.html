Privacy Notice — HockeyCut

Not legal advice. Version EN-PRV-1.2-draft · 24 November 2026. Square brackets [...] are placeholders. Turkish users: see the Turkish notice (KVKK Aydınlatma Metni).

1. In short

  • Your match video is not sent to us. It is read straight into your computer from the file on it or from your own Google Drive. League matches are analysed once on our side and the app downloads that ready analysis; where there is none and the video is a file on your computer, the analysis runs on your computer. We cannot see videos kept on your computer. The Google Drive permission is read-only; Google makes this permission type cover all files in your Drive, the app reads only the video you select, and you can remove the permission in your Google account at any time.
  • What reaches us: the Yes/No answers you give, short clips of those moments, and numeric event data (the Training Contribution). Also: your account, licence and support data (and payment data once paid plans are on sale).
  • Why: we use the Training Contribution only to train, test and improve our models. It is the way we pay for offering the app for free or at a low price. We do not sell it or publish it.
  • People in the clips: clips can show players, referees and others, including minors. We have no way to contact them. This notice is also for them (Section 6).
  • No facial recognition. We do not create face templates or voiceprints and do not identify people.
  • No ads, no sale of data, no tracking of your site visits by us. (The website host, GitHub, keeps its own access logs.)
  • Your rights: see Section 10. Contact: destek@hockeycut.com.

6. If you appear in a clip (players, referees, parents, others)

6.1. A person who uses our app sent a short clip that shows you. We did not collect it from you. We do not know who you are: we do not receive names or shirt-number lists, and we do not use facial recognition. We cannot contact you.

6.2. We use the clip only to train, test and improve our models. We keep it encrypted, for at most [24 months].

6.3. The app user has declared to us that they have the right to use the video and that they asked for permission from the people shown (and from the parents or legal guardians of minors). We rely on this, but it does not replace your rights.

6.4. You can ask us to delete a clip that shows you, or object to its use, by writing to destek@hockeycut.com. Please tell us enough to find the clip (for example, the match, the date, the team, the time). We may ask for information to help us find it. If we cannot find a clip that shows you, we will tell you.

6.5. A parent or legal guardian can ask on behalf of a minor.

6.6. Because we cannot identify you, some rights (such as access or portability) can only be met if you give us information that lets us find your data (GDPR Art. 11).

7. Children

7.1. The app is for adults (18+). We do not offer it to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child directly.

7.2. Clips may show minors (for example, youth players). The adult who loads the video declares that they have the permission of a parent or legal guardian. We treat clips with minors with extra care: short clips, reduced quality, no names, no sound, encryption, limited access, and quick deletion on request.

7.3. If you think a child is using the app, write to destek@hockeycut.com. We will close the account and delete the data.

11. Security and incidents

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and logging, separation of the stored clips from the web, and limits on who can read them. If a data breach is likely to risk people's rights, we notify the competent authority without undue delay and where possible within 72 hours, and we tell affected people where the law requires it.

12. AI models

We use the Training Contribution to build models that find events in hockey video. A trained model is not a copy of the clips, but it may be affected by them, and it cannot always be changed to remove one piece of data. We do not claim that a model "forgets" a clip. We delete the clips themselves on valid requests and stop using them for future training.

13. Cookies and similar technology

Our website and portal use only strictly necessary cookies and local storage (a login session, a two-step sign-in step, your language choice). We do not use advertising, analytics or social-media trackers, and we do not load fonts or scripts from third parties. If we add analytics later, we will update this notice and ask for consent where the law requires.
